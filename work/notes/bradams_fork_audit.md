The report does not establish why a user-visible index of 0 becomes argument 206. It is a valuable investigation lead, not proof that Bradams shipped a robust PC fix. ## Implications for this repository

This report is an external-fork inventory, not authorization to adopt its behavior changes. Under D-1002 and D-1337, gameplay behavior remains anchored to untouched Chinese v4.0.3 and original hack logic bugs remain report-only. Hard mode, caps, timing changes, affection changes and engine bug repairs need to be considered under those existing decisions. Nickname and English UI repairs may overlap work already present here, but their equivalence needs actual comparison.

Before attributing a binary repair to this fork, establish the exact translation base, compare relevant ARM9/overlay/script/resource changes, and reproduce the same scenario on untouched Chinese, our matching translation build and the fork. Preserve the distinction between a translation regression, an original hack bug and an intentional fork feature.
